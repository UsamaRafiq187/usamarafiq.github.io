Overview

Insure Verify AI is an AI-assisted verification platform for car rental operators — confirming a renter's identity, driver's license and insurance in seconds. It runs tiered subscriptions with a SOC 2 Type II posture and AES-256 encryption end-to-end, on native iOS with a hardened Laravel API. I built the iOS app that turns a slow, manual approval into an instant one.
