About me

From my first shipped screen to owning apps end-to-end.

I started at Codelps as an iOS intern and grew into a Senior iOS Developer — today I lead iOS delivery at Rent FYV and Codelps for founders across the US, UK, and the Gulf. My work sits where clean engineering meets thoughtful UX — Swift underneath, interfaces that feel effortless on top.

I've shipped apps across car rental, fintech, connectivity, e-commerce, and healthcare — and these days I'm bringing SwiftUI and on-device AI into new work. I don't just ship features; I ask why they matter, how they'll scale, and what makes them feel right for the person using them.
