    About

    Five years on one platform

    I'm Usama Rafiq, a Senior iOS Developer with 5+ years of building apps that real customers install. I started at Codelps as an intern and grew into leading iOS delivery at Rent FYV and Codelps for founders across the US, UK and the Gulf.

    Most of my work is Swift with UIKit and SwiftUI where they fit — bookings and payments for car rental, AI identity checks for operators, and right-to-left Urdu storefronts for wholesalers, plus the push, maps and analytics underneath.

    I stay with an app after launch. Rent FYV, Insure Verify AI and Surgicrafts keep shipping updates long after day one.
